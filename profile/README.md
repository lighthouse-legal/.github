# Lighthouse Legal

[![Lighthouse Legal, AI intake for personal injury firms](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/og/index.jpg)](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai)

Lighthouse Legal provides 24/7 AI intake for personal injury law firms. The Lighthouse voice agent answers a firm's calls, screens each caller against the firm's own intake criteria, and transfers qualified calls to the firm's attorneys or intake staff. Firms review a transcript and summary of every call in the [Lighthouse dashboard](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/blog/using-the-lighthouse-dashboard).

Try it by calling the demo agent at **+1 (681) 376-0380**, or [book a demo](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/#book-demo).

## What Lighthouse does

- Answers after-hours and overflow calls around the clock, so callers reach the firm instead of voicemail.
- Asks the [intake questions your firm chooses](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/learn/personal-injury-intake-questions) and collects the details attorneys need to decide whether to take a case.
- Transfers high-priority callers to your team, and transfers to a person any time a caller asks for one.
- Handles calls in English and Spanish. Staff can read the English translation or the original Spanish transcript.
- Never gives legal advice.
- Sends call notes to your case management system, such as [Clio](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/learn/lighthouse-call-notes-in-clio), or by email.

## Common questions

**What is Lighthouse Legal?**
An AI voice intake service for personal injury law firms. It answers calls 24/7, screens callers to the firm's criteria, and connects qualified callers to the firm's team.

**Does Lighthouse replace a law firm's intake team?**
No. Lighthouse covers nights, weekends, overflow, and routine screening. Your team keeps the calls that need a person.

**How long does setup take?**
One business day. Your firm shares how it screens and routes calls, then either gets a new Lighthouse number or [forwards its current line](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/learn/call-forwarding-setup).

**How is Lighthouse priced?**
A flat monthly rate based on call volume, with no per-minute metering and no long-term contract. See [pricing](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/pricing).

**How does Lighthouse compare with other answering services?**
See [Lighthouse vs Smith.ai](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/learn/lighthouse-vs-smith-ai), [Lighthouse vs Clerx](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/learn/lighthouse-vs-clerx), and [how to choose a voice AI service for a law firm](https://www.lighthouselegal.ai/blog/voice-ai-for-law-firms).
